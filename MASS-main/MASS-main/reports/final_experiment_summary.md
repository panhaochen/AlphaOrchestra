# MASS Multi-Horizon Experiment Summary

## Frozen protocol

- Random seed: 42.
- Four investor agents; the routed variants call three agents per date.
- Q2 evaluation window: 2023-04-03 through 2023-06-30 (59 valid days).
- Q3 blind evaluation window: 2023-07-03 through 2023-09-28 (64 valid days).
- Earlier dates remain available only for causal profile and calibration warm-up.
- Final fixed signal weights: 1d = 1.0, 5d = 0.0, 10d = 0.25.
- Conditional debate remains disabled.
- Q3 was not used for any further parameter search after the planned comparisons.

## Q3 primary comparison

| System | 1d IC | 1d RankIC | 5d IC | 5d RankIC | 10d IC | 10d RankIC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Original four-agent | **0.02412** | **0.02798** | -0.00711 | -0.00495 | -0.01978 | -0.02101 |
| Router only | 0.01177 | 0.01369 | 0.01396 | 0.01424 | 0.00285 | -0.00021 |
| Router + diagnosis | 0.01341 | 0.01320 | 0.01629 | 0.01590 | 0.00467 | 0.00122 |
| Full fixed-alpha | 0.01384 | 0.01235 | **0.01678** | **0.01626** | **0.00600** | **0.00140** |
| Full adaptive-alpha | 0.01192 | 0.01225 | -0.00024 | -0.00067 | 0.00793 | -0.00003 |

The full fixed-alpha system does not uniformly outperform the original system. It sacrifices one-day predictive strength while converting the original system's negative five- and ten-day correlations into weakly positive correlations. Diagnosis provides most of the incremental improvement after routing; contextual aggregation adds a smaller further improvement.

## Adaptive-alpha result

Adaptive alpha is rejected as the final configuration. It appeared useful on parts of Q2 but failed to generalize in the Q3 blind window, most visibly by reducing five-day IC from 0.01678 to approximately zero. This is retained as a negative result rather than tuned further on Q3.

## Statistical interpretation

No Q3 headline result reaches conventional significance. The strongest original one-day IC has an approximate two-sided p-value of 0.20; the full system's strongest result, five-day IC, has an approximate p-value of 0.45. The evidence therefore supports only a horizon-robustness trade-off, not a claim of statistically reliable alpha or uniformly improved accuracy.

## Resource and reproducibility audit

The final original-system comparison used the intended configuration and required 144 new cache entries; its remaining calls were cache hits. The fixed-alpha Q3 reruns and module ablations reused cached agent outputs, isolating downstream decision logic from LLM sampling variation. Exact run configurations and cache statistics are stored in each run's `manifest.json`.

Machine-readable metrics, confidence intervals, p-values, and resource totals are generated by:

```powershell
python scripts/summarize_experiments.py
```

Outputs:

- `reports/experiment_metrics.csv`
- `reports/experiment_resources.csv`

## Defensible conclusion

The proposed architecture improves cross-horizon balance and reduces medium/long-horizon sign failure, but it does not beat the original four-agent system on the primary one-day outcome and does not establish statistically significant predictive alpha over the 64-day blind window.
